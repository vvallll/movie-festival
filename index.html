Welcome to Vesper Film Festival

Vesper is an independent film festival celebrating intimate, atmospheric, and thought-provoking cinema in London. Our programme brings together short films, feature films, and documentaries from emerging and established filmmakers from around the world.

Join us for a unique cinematic experience that explores the art of storytelling through the lens of independent filmmaking. Discover new voices, engage with filmmakers, and immerse yourself in the world of cinema at Vesper Film Festival.

As our closing tradition, we invite the participating filmmakers to choose a classic film that means something to them. We watch their selections together in a much more relaxed atmosphere — no competition, no judging, no expectations. Just good films, good company, and a late evening spent talking about cinema.

About the Festival

Founded in 2026, Vesper was created as a space for independent cinema, new voices, and genuine conversations around film.

We celebrate the filmmakers of today while keeping close to the films that inspired them — because cinema is not only something to watch, but something to share.

We believe cinema is about curiosity, connection, and the joy of watching something together.
